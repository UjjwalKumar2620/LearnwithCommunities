About This Tracker

Google Cloud Learn with Communities

A program by Google Cloud that helps students and developers learn cloud skills through structured learning paths, hands-on labs, and community engagement. Participants earn skill badges by completing courses and labs on Google Cloud Skills Boost.

GDG BPIT

Google Developer Groups on Campus at Bhagwan Parshuram Institute of Technology (BPIT), New Delhi. We are a community of students passionate about Google technologies, cloud computing, AI/ML, and software development.

Milestones

Participants progress through milestones by earning skill badges. Milestone 2 requires 5 badges, and Milestone 3 requires 10 badges. Each milestone unlocks achievements and recognition within the community.

This Tracker

This website tracks the progress of all GDG BPIT participants in the Google Cloud Learn with Communities program. You can search for participants, view their skill badges, and see the leaderboard rankings based on badges earned.
